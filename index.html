Hostel Rules & Guidelines

Simple rules that keep the living environment safe, clean, and peaceful for everyone.

Timings & Visitors

  • Main gate closes at 10:30 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends; late entry requires prior warden approval.
  • Visitors are allowed only in the reception/visitor lounge between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM.
  • Overnight stays by guests or non-residents are strictly prohibited.
  • Residents must inform the warden before leaving for overnight trips or vacations.

Conduct, Safety & Billing

  • Quiet hours are observed from 10:30 PM to 6:30 AM; use headphones for music/videos.
  • Smoking, alcohol, illegal substances, and high-wattage cooking appliances in rooms are not permitted.
  • Keep rooms and washrooms tidy; report maintenance issues promptly.
  • Monthly rent is due by the 5th of every month; 30 days' notice is required before vacating.
